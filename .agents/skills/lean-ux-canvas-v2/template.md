# Lean UX Canvas v2 Template

Capture the eight boxes. Box 2 carries both the money trail and the metric.

## Template
```markdown
## Lean UX Canvas v2
Initiative: [title]  ·  Date: [date]  ·  Iteration: [n]

### 1. Business Problem
- [What changed? Why is this a problem now?]
- **Assumption to validate:** [one unvalidated assumption / how we may be part of the problem]

### 2. Business Outcomes
- **Money trail:** Stakeholders → [role each] · Path: [payer]→[…intermediaries…]→[capturer] · Final money-capturer: [who keeps the money when this succeeds]
- **Status-quo beneficiary:** [who benefits if the problem persists / who loses if it's solved]
- **Outcome metric:** [measurable behavior change — baseline→target, timeframe, segment — that moves money toward the final money-capturer]
  <!-- One metric per hop between the behavior and the money-capturer. If the behavior IS the money event, one line. If money is downstream, add: -->
  <!-- - Leading: [behavior you measure — baseline→target, timeframe, segment] -->
  <!-- - Lagging: [money it captures → +$ to which capturer] -->
  <!-- - Link (assume, validate): [why the leading change produces the money — viability risk, candidate for Box 7] -->

### 3. Users
- [Primary persona to focus on first]

### 4. User Outcomes & Benefits
- [User goals, benefits, emotions — why they'd seek this]

### 5. Solutions
- [3+ candidate solutions to explore]

### 6. Hypotheses
- We believe [outcome] will be achieved if [user] attains [benefit] with [solution].

### 7. Riskiest Assumption
- [Single riskiest assumption right now, and its risk type: value / usability / feasibility / viability]

### 8. Least Work / Experiment
- [Smallest experiment (<2 weeks) to validate or invalidate the riskiest assumption — probe matched to the Box 7 risk type]
- **Disposal:** [delete-by date + how you'll dispose of the probe]
```
