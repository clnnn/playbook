# Lean Product Canvas (v3) Template

Capture the eight boxes. Box 2 carries both the money trail and the key-result metric ("who does what by how much"). Box 4 is a Jobs-to-be-Done pass. Box 7 prioritizes hypotheses by risk × perceived value.

## Template
```markdown
## Lean Product Canvas (v3)
Title of initiative: [title]  ·  Date: [date]  ·  Iteration: [n]

### 1. Business Problem
- [What changed in the market/customers/technology? Why is this the most important problem now?]
- **Assumption to validate:** [one unvalidated assumption / how we may be part of the problem]

### 2. Business Outcomes
- **Money trail:** Stakeholders → [role each] · Path: [payer]→[…intermediaries…]→[capturer] · Final money-capturer: [who keeps the money when this succeeds]
- **Status-quo beneficiary:** [who benefits if the problem persists / who loses if it's solved]
- **Outcome (key results):** [who — does what — by how much: segment, behavior change, baseline→target, timeframe — moving money toward the final money-capturer]
  <!-- One key result per hop between the behavior and the money-capturer. If the behavior IS the money event, one line. If money is downstream, add: -->
  <!-- - Leading: [who does what by how much — the behavior you measure] -->
  <!-- - Lagging: [money it captures → +$ to which capturer] -->
  <!-- - Link (assume, validate): [why the leading change produces the money — viability risk, candidate for Box 7] -->

### 3. Users
- [Primary persona to focus on first — who buys / uses / configures / is impacted]

### 4. User Outcomes & Benefits (JTBD)
- **Job (When…I want to…so I can…):** [the core Job To Be Done, solution-agnostic]
- **Functional job:** [task to complete]
- **Social / emotional job:** [how they want to be seen / the state they want to feel or avoid]
- **Most acute pain:** [the sharpest obstacle, cost, mistake, or unresolved problem]
- **Most-wanted gain:** [the savings / improvement that would drive adoption]

### 5. Solutions
- [3+ candidate product/feature/enhancement ideas to explore]

### 6. Hypotheses
- We believe [outcome] will be achieved if [user] attains [benefit] with [feature].

### 7. Learning Priorities
- **Prioritization (risk × perceived value):** [which hypothesis is high value + high risk → the Test quadrant, test first]
- **Riskiest assumption:** [single riskiest assumption right now, and its risk type: value / usability / feasibility / viability]

### 8. Experiments
- [Smallest experiment (<2 weeks; 1 day / 1 week / 1 month) to validate or invalidate the riskiest assumption — probe matched to the Box 7 risk type, with a pass/fail threshold]
- **Disposal:** [delete-by date + how you'll dispose of the probe]
```
